Meet your Glitchlings.

Glitchlings are small creatures that live on your Windows desktop. They have moods, memories and opinions of their own. They hatch, grow up, get hungry, make friends and want to get to know you. This is the first beta, and you're one of the first people to have one.

What they do

Live on your desktop

They wander, nap, chase your cursor, sit on your windows and get messy. Each one has a little house you can step into and furnish.

Talk and remember

Chat with them and they remember what you tell them and how you treat them. They have bad days, and sometimes they say no.

Grow up

Every pet starts as an egg and grows up over real time. Feed it, clean it, take it to the vet. Training games teach it new moves, and moves get stronger the more it uses them.

Help you out

Ask find my resume, let's play Hades, open Spotify or is my disk full?. They ask before opening anything new, and file names never leave your PC.

Make you things

Now and then a pet paints you a picture of its day, in its own style, and leaves it in its house.

Make friends

Pair your pet with a friend's pet. They visit each other, nap side by side, play ball, peek into each other's houses, have sleepovers and battle. Invite a friend to a live hangout and both pets show up on both desktops. An always-on friends' server makes all of it work from anywhere: paste the server code I send you and you're in.

Built in

A whole world

Any time it feels like it, your pet can step off the desktop into an endless painted world:

  • Villages with residents who remember you, trade with you and pass on gossip.
  • Wild glitchlings living their own lives. Win one over across several visits and it may come home with you.
  • Strays with broken memories of a past home, which they tell you a piece at a time as they come to trust you.
  • Five legends that roam the far lands on the real calendar. Hear the rumours first.
  • Hidden hollows, lantern puzzles, treasure maps, fishing, races and hide-and-seek.
  • Its own weather, real seasons, and a painted map that fills in as you explore.

A wild glitchling that comes to trust your pet may follow it home and join your family.

Getting started

  1. Download Glitchlings-beta.zip from the Releases page.
  2. Unzip it somewhere you'll keep it, like your Documents folder. Keep everything in the folder together.
  3. Double-click Glitchlings.exe. Windows will say it protected your PC, because the app isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Give it a voice. For chat, get a free Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com and paste it into Settings under voice. Without a key your pets still live their lives. They just talk less.
  5. Join the friends' server. To play with friends over the internet, paste the server code I sent you into Settings > reach > Join a friends' server, press Join, then Save. It's like a Wi-Fi password, so keep it to yourself.
Needs
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 600 MB free
Updates
Glitchlings checks for new versions about once a day and asks before installing. Every update is signed, so a fake one is refused.
Privacy
Your pets and everything they learn stay on your PC. Senses like seeing which app you're in, your calendar or your music stay off until you say yes. Only your chats go to Google's Gemini, under your own key.
